Our Projects

Exploring the intersection of engineering disciplines through hands-on builds.

Autonomous Systems & AI

We work on projects involving machine learning and autonomous navigation, such as AWS DeepRacer. Members get to experiment with reinforcement learning models and see them perform in real-time environments.

Technical Workshops

From hardware prototyping with Arduino to custom PCB design, our workshops allow members to gain industry-relevant skills. We focus on building a strong foundation in both electrical and mechanical engineering principles.
